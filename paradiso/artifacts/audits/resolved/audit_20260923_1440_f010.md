## 2. ADVERSARIAL RE-VERIFICATION & EVIDENCE

### ATTACK VECTOR 1: CUTOFF TERMINATION OF RE-RUN REPORTS (`services/intraday_service.py:339`)

**OBSERVATION**  
In `IntradayService._close_day()`, the termination filter previously skipped in-flight reports that had an entry in `day.reports_ran`. In the updated code, the loop evaluates:
```python
if r.name in running_reports or (r.name not in already_ran and r.status != "Completed"):
```
Active running reports in `running_reports` are explicitly matched regardless of whether they have entries in `already_ran`.

**EVIDENCE**  
In `poc_f010_verification.py` (`test_2200_cutoff_rerun_task_killed_and_marked_failed`):
1. A report was executed, recorded in `day.reports_ran`, and then re-spawned into `current_runs` prior to 22:00 cutoff.
2. `_close_day()` was triggered.
3. The underlying OS process was terminated via `Runner.kill_all()`.
4. `current_runs` was emptied (`len == 0`).
5. Automation status in `storage/automations.json` transitioned cleanly to `"Failed"` with `"breached 10:00 PM cutoff (exceeded grace window)"`.

---

### ATTACK VECTOR 2: MIDNIGHT ROLLOVER ON PRE-EXISTING DAY RECORD (`services/intraday_service.py:246`)

**OBSERVATION**  
In `IntradayService`, active date tracking via `self._active_date` decouples midnight rollover detection from storage record absence:
```python
is_new_day = (today_date != self._active_date)
if not day or is_new_day:
    self._active_date = today_date
```
When crossing midnight into a date whose record was pre-seeded in `storage/intraday.json`, the condition `is_new_day` evaluates to `True`.

**EVIDENCE**  
In `poc_f010_verification.py` (`test_midnight_rollover_kills_strays_on_pre_existing_day_record`):
1. A future day record was pre-created in `intraday.json`.
2. A stray process was spawned into `current_runs`.
3. Clock was advanced to the future date, and `tick()` was executed.
4. Stray process was terminated by `kill_all()`, `current_runs` cleared, past days reconciled, and automations reset to `"Waiting"`.
5. Builder unit test `test_f010_cutoff_kills_running_tasks_and_rollover_is_clean` passes across all test runs.

### ATTACK VECTOR 3: QUEUE DEDUPLICATION ACROSS DAY ROLLOVERS

**OBSERVATION**  
During waitlist deck initialization and periodic reconciliation, in-flight tasks and existing waitlist members are filtered out:

**EVIDENCE**
